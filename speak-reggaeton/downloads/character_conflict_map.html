Two speaking roles throughout all twelve main dialogues

Character & Conflict Map

The book has two principal speaking characters. Messages from friends in other regions are quoted, but those senders are not additional dialogue roles.

Nora

Role: Plans the neighbourhood music night and helps shape the invitation, event programme and spoken introduction.

Strengths: Organised, observant, attentive to how language can be understood by different listeners.

Challenges: Worrying about the event, interpreting silent messages and finding a clear, confident speaking pace.

Luis

Role: Makes the demo recording, handles audio details and shares musical decisions.

Strengths: Enthusiastic, creative, practical about explaining music through examples.

Challenges: Too many track choices, an unclear post claiming credit and a missing file on opening day.

Shared goal

Neighbourhood reggaeton night in San Juan

Nora + Luis → rehearsal → poster → playlist → recording → dance programme → video → concert preparation → opening night.

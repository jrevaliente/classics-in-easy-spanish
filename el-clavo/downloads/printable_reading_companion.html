Printable Reading Companion

Use these prompts after each chapter or on a second reading. Story first.

Chapter 1 — El número 1

Note three details that make the unnamed traveller seem mysterious before Felipe knows anything about her past.

Chapter 2 — Una mujer sin nombre

Why does her refusal to give Felipe a name matter more than a simple farewell?

Chapter 3 — Otro viaje

What has changed in Zarco since Felipe last knew him, and what does that suggest before the full story is told?

Chapter 4 — Blanca

Track the contradiction: Blanca says she is a widow, but says she cannot marry Zarco. What possibilities can a reader consider at this point?

Chapter 5 — La promesa y la desaparición

Separate what Zarco knows from what he assumes after Blanca disappears. Then note what changes when the skull and nail are found.

Chapter 6 — Primeras diligencias

Reconstruct the identification chain from the coffin board to Alfonso and Gabriela without skipping a step.

Chapter 7 — Mercedes de Méridanueva

What does Felipe recognise, and what still remains unknown after the Granada encounter?

Chapter 8 — La Fonda del León

Why is the location of Blanca and Gabriela the key fair-play clue before the formal reveal?

Chapter 9 — El juicio

Distinguish motive, cause and responsibility. What did Zarco know, and what did Gabriela decide on her own?

Chapter 10 — Último viaje

Explain why the pardon arrives in time to stop the execution but not in time to save Gabriela.

Read Again Challenge

On a second reading, identify the earliest clues that become meaningful only after the courtroom reveal. Which clues were fair without giving the solution away?

Character & Evidence Map

Major spoiler warning: this map reveals identities and the solution. Use it after finishing the story.

Person / evidenceRole in the mystery
FelipeNarrator and observer. He first meets the unnamed traveller, later recognises her as Mercedes, and notices the Fonda del León coincidence before the courtroom reveal.
Joaquín ZarcoFelipe's friend and judge. He tells the story of Blanca, investigates Alfonso's death, and must finally judge the woman he loves.
Gabriela Zahara del ValleThe woman's true legal identity; wife of Alfonso. She is also the woman Zarco knew as Blanca, the traveller Felipe met without a name, and Mercedes de Méridanueva.
BlancaThe identity used with Zarco in Seville. She claims to be a widow and repeatedly refuses marriage before finally agreeing.
Mercedes de MéridanuevaThe identity used in Granada when Felipe recognises the traveller again.
Don Alfonso Gutiérrez del RomeralGabriela's husband and the murder victim identified through the coffin evidence and parish record.
Skull + iron nailTransforms the love story into a criminal investigation and establishes that Alfonso did not die naturally.
A. G. R. / 1843 / R. I. P.Coffin-board evidence that leads Zarco to the burial registers.
Parish burial recordIdentifies Alfonso, the date of burial, the original apoplexy explanation and his wife Gabriela.
Servants + doctorsPlace Gabriela with Alfonso on the night of death and establish that the nail wound could have looked like apoplexy.
Fonda del LeónThe fair-play clue connecting Blanca's return with Gabriela's capture before the courtroom confirmation.

Evidence chain

Skull + nail → coffin marks → burial register → Alfonso → Gabriela → witness/medical evidence → Fonda del León → courtroom recognition → confession.

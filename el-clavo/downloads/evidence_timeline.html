Evidence Timeline

Spoiler warning: use this resource after finishing the story, or only when you want to review the complete chain of clues.

15 April 1843 — Zarco leaves Seville after Blanca agrees to marry him and asks him to return by 15 May.

30 April 1843 — Zarco returns early. Blanca has disappeared from the Seville inn.

Night before 4 May 1843 — Alfonso Gutiérrez del Romeral dies. The death is accepted at first as apoplexy.

4 May 1843 — Alfonso's burial is recorded. Zarco leaves Seville for Madrid, still believing Blanca has deceived him.

Autumn 1844 — Felipe travels from Granada to Málaga and meets the mysterious woman in mourning who refuses to give her name.

1 November 1844 — Felipe reaches the unnamed Córdoba-province town and reunites with Zarco. At the cemetery, the skull pierced by an iron nail is discovered.

Investigation — Coffin marks A. G. R. / 1843 / R. I. P. lead to the parish burial record, Alfonso's identity and the name of his wife, Gabriela Zahara del Valle.

That winter — In Granada, Felipe recognises the mysterious traveller again, now presented as Mercedes de Méridanueva.

Shortly afterwards — Zarco is summoned to the Fonda del León and reunites with Blanca. Gabriela is then captured at the same inn.

The judicial confrontation — Zarco recognises Gabriela as Blanca; Felipe recognises the complete identity chain. The skull and nail trigger Gabriela's reaction and confession.

Twenty days after sentence — The Audiencia de Sevilla confirms the death sentence.

Execution morning — Zarco arrives with an official pardon. The execution is stopped, but Gabriela dies before she can leave alive.

Later — Zarco rebuilds his life as a magistrate in Havana, marries and has a son.

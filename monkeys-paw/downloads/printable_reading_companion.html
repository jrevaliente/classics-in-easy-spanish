The Monkey’s Paw · B1 with support · W. W. Jacobs

Name: _________________________   Date: _____________

Before reading

Predict what three wishes could mean for an ordinary family. Write one sentence in Spanish.

What atmosphere might a rainy night and a warm home create?

Parte I — Read · Listen · Respond

Listening focus: Listen for the contrast between the storm and the warm room.

Comprensión

  1. ¿Quién juega al ajedrez y quién teje junto al fuego?
  2. ¿Por qué Morris intenta quemar la pata?
  3. ¿Qué cantidad pide el señor White?

Vocabulary in context

hechizo · advertencia · retorcerse · brasas

Use two of these words in original Spanish sentences.

Writing task

Explain an ignored warning using porque and sin embargo.

Parte II — Read · Listen · Respond

Listening focus: Notice the visitor’s hesitation and changes in tone.

Comprensión

  1. ¿Por qué Herbert sale de casa por la mañana?
  2. ¿Qué indica que el visitante vacila?
  3. ¿Cómo se relacionan las doscientas libras con la familia?

Vocabulary in context

coincidencia · vacilar · compensación · hebra

Use two of these words in original Spanish sentences.

Writing task

List two observations and one character interpretation.

Parte III — Read · Listen · Respond

Listening focus: Compare the mother’s determination with the father’s dread.

Comprensión

  1. ¿Cómo cambia la vida de los padres después del entierro?
  2. ¿Qué quiere la señora White?
  3. ¿Qué no se explica al final?

Vocabulary in context

cerrojo · desesperación · susurrar · consuelo

Use two of these words in original Spanish sentences.

Writing task

Propose two explanations of the final knocks, identifying what remains unknown.

After reading all three parts

  1. Which decision has the greatest consequence? Justify.
  2. Write three observed facts and three beliefs or interpretations.
  3. Write a spoiler-free 50-word recommendation for another B1 learner.
  4. What remains ambiguous in the last scene?

For model answers, full contextual glossary and the complete English translation, consult the printed or electronic book.

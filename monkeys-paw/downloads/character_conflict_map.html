Character & Conflict Map

Full-story spoilers. Use after Parts I–III.

Central object

THE MONKEY’S PAW

It represents three possible wishes, but its powers are never independently verified.

↓
Curiosity · Warning · Family loss · Uncertainty

Who wants what?

MR WHITE

Curiosity · fear · responsibility

Keeps the paw and makes the wishes. Must respond to his wife's grief and his own dread.

MRS WHITE

Skepticism · grief · determination

Questions the paw at first; then insists on a wish she hopes may restore her son.

HERBERT

Humor · absence

His joking role gives way to an absence that transforms his parents’ home.

SERGEANT-MAJOR MORRIS

Knowledge · warning

Brings the object, tells its history and tries to destroy it. His warnings are not proof.

VISITOR FROM MAW AND MEGGINS

News · compensation

Reports what his employer says and the compensation offered; he witnesses no magic.

Three evidence checks

  1. What does Morris assert? What is shown?
  2. What does the visitor actually confirm?
  3. What do the parents hear, and what stays unseen?

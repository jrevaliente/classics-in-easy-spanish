Three-part story · spoiler guide

Conflict Timeline

Contains spoilers for Parts I, II and III. Read the story first to preserve the suspense.

I · Morris’s warning

Morris describes the paw and warns Mr White against its use.

Observation or interpretation?

His testimony is a warning, not proof of supernatural power.

I · The first wish

Mr White wishes for two hundred pounds and later reports feeling the paw twist.

Observation or interpretation?

The wish is explicit, but the meaning of the reported movement is unresolved.

II · An uncomfortable message

A visitor from Maw and Meggins arrives with news.

Observation or interpretation?

After Herbert’s death, the family is offered exactly two hundred pounds as compensation.

III · The second wish

At Mrs White’s insistence, Mr White wishes for Herbert’s return. Knocking follows.

Observation or interpretation?

Nothing confirms the identity or even the source of the knocks.

III · The third wish

Mr White whispers a wish. The knocking stops; the street is empty.

Observation or interpretation?

The exact words and the cause of the silence remain unknown.

Reading challenge

Does the paw cause the events, or does the family interpret events through the wishes? Distinguish what a character claims from what is directly narrated. The ending does not settle this question.

How to use this companion

Read first. Enjoy the Spanish story without trying to understand every word. Listen next. Replay the matching part as you follow the text. Use the vocabulary and English translation in the book when helpful.

For independent learning, aim to follow the plot and characters before reviewing individual words. The story comes first.

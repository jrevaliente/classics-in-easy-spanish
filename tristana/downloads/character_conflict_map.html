Four conflict axes

Autonomy vs dependence: Tristana wants to earn her living without belonging to another person.

Protection vs power: Don Lope’s generosity coexists with manipulation.

Love vs different futures: Tristana and Horacio have diverging aspirations.

Change vs certainty: The narrative refuses an easy explanation of its ambiguous ending.

Conflict Timeline

Before Batiste arrives — The Barret property carries the memory of an earlier conflict over land, poverty and eviction. The abandoned barraca has become a symbol inside the huerta community.

Arrival — Batiste and his family move onto the abandoned land. Their attempt to begin again is met with silent hostility from neighbours.

Rebuilding — The family repairs the barraca and works the neglected fields. Their visible progress does not remove the social rejection around them.

Water conflict — Access to irrigation water becomes a practical struggle for survival and a new source of confrontation.

Escalation — Batiste's efforts to defend his family increasingly pull him into the same cycle of anger and violence that destroyed the people who came before him.

Pascualet's death — Personal tragedy deepens the family's isolation. The funeral procession becomes one of the emotional centres of the story.

The tavern fight — The conflict with Pimentó turns openly violent inside Copa's tavern.

Final fire — The barraca burns. The family is displaced while the surrounding community watches without coming to help.

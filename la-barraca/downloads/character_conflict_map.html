Character & Conflict Map

PersonRole in the conflict
BatisteTenant farmer who brings his family to the abandoned property and tries to rebuild a life through work.
TeresaBatiste's wife; shares the family's labour, fear and losses.
RosetaOne of Batiste and Teresa's children; part of the family unit exposed to the hostility of the huerta.
BatistetSon of Batiste and Teresa; helps with work around the property.
PascualetYoung child in the family whose death becomes a major tragedy.
PimentóPowerful and feared man of the huerta; a central social antagonist in Batiste's conflict with the community.
PepetaConnected to the local community and to Pimentó; participates in the social world surrounding the family.
Tío TombaOld shepherd who warns Batiste that he is repeating the destructive path of Barret.
Don JoaquínTeacher connected with the children and the funeral procession.
BarretPrevious farmer whose history still shapes the meaning of the land and the community's hostility.

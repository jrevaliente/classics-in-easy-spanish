Printable Reading Companion

Before you read

What does a home mean when the land around it is controlled by a hostile community?

Track the family

For each chapter, note one practical problem Batiste's family faces and one emotional consequence.

Track the huerta

Write down details about the barracas, paths, acequias, crops, work, water and local customs.

Track the conflict

When does disagreement become exclusion? When does exclusion become violence?

Character contrast

Compare Batiste and Pimentó. What gives each man power? What does each man fear losing?

Read Again Challenge

After the final chapter, return to the family's arrival. Which early details already suggest that rebuilding the barraca will not be enough to make them part of the community?

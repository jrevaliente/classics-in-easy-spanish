Character & World Map

Character / groupRole in the story
Claus / Santa ClausHuman child adopted in Burzee who chooses to devote his life to bringing joy to children.
NecileWood Nymph who rescues, adopts and raises Claus as her son.
AkMaster of the Woods; introduces Claus to the human world and later argues for his immortality.
Queen ZurlineQueen of the Wood Nymphs; supports Necile and Claus.
RylsImmortal beings who care for plants and flowers; they help with food, colors and later Claus’s work.
KnooksImmortal guardians of animals; they protect Claus and regulate his use of the reindeer.
FairiesImmortal protectors of humans who repeatedly help Claus, including recovering stolen toys.
ShiegraLioness who protects and feeds the abandoned baby before Necile takes him into Burzee.
Flossie & GlossieThe first two reindeer to pull Claus’s sleigh.
WeekumChild rescued during a snowstorm; his love for the wooden cat helps Claus understand the value of toys.
MayrieChild whose fear of the wooden lion teaches Claus that toys should bring joy, not fear.
Bessie BlithesomeRich child whose request makes Claus decide that his toys should be for all children.
The AwgwasHostile beings who try to stop Claus because the toys weaken their influence over children.
Kilter, Peter, Nuter & WiskLater magical helpers who support Santa as the world and his work expand.

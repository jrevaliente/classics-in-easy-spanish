Evidence Timeline

Spoiler warning: this timeline reveals the full story. Use it after reading or for a second-reading review.

1747 — Rev. Abijah Hoadley preaches about strange voices beneath Sentinel Hill; he later disappears.

2 Feb 1913 — Wilbur Whateley is born. His father is not identified.

1913 onward — Old Whateley repeatedly buys cattle, but the herd does not grow in proportion.

1913–1914 — Wilbur grows at an impossible rate; the tool-shed and upper farmhouse are sealed and altered.

1917 — Outside investigators and reporters visit the Whateley family, but no decisive explanation is found.

Lammas Night 1924 — Old Whateley dies after giving Wilbur final instructions about more space, feeding, page 751 and Yog-Sothoth.

Hallowe’en 1926 — Lavinia Whateley disappears.

Summer 1927 — Wilbur moves books and possessions to the sheds and seals the lower part of the house.

1928 — Wilbur visits Miskatonic University to consult the complete Necronomicon; Armitage refuses to lend it.

Night 2–3 Aug 1928 — Wilbur breaks into the Miskatonic library, is attacked by the watchdog, and dies. His hidden anatomy is revealed.

2 Sep 1928 — Armitage breaks the main barrier in Wilbur’s cipher and begins to understand the diary.

Night 9 Sep 1928 — The hidden Horror escapes from the Whateley farmhouse.

10–14 Sep 1928 — Tracks, ruined farms, dead cattle and crushed vegetation prove that an enormous invisible being is moving through Dunwich.

15 Sep 1928 — The Horror moves toward Sentinel Hill. Powder makes it briefly visible.

15 Sep 1928 — Armitage, Rice and Morgan use the prepared formula; the entity calls to Father and Yog-Sothoth and is destroyed or sent back.

Final revelation — Armitage explains that the Horror was Wilbur’s twin and looked more like their father.

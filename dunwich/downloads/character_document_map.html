Character & Document Map

Spoiler warning: this map is designed for review after the story.

Person / sourceContribution
Wilbur WhateleyCentral figure whose abnormal growth, studies and search for the Necronomicon drive the first half of the plot.
Lavinia WhateleyWilbur’s mother; daughter of Old Whateley. Her pregnancy and later disappearance remain key family mysteries.
Old WhateleyLavinia’s father; raises and instructs Wilbur, buys cattle, alters the farmhouse and transfers crucial instructions before death.
Henry ArmitageMiskatonic librarian and scholar who recognizes the danger, deciphers Wilbur’s diary and leads the final response.
Warren RiceMiskatonic professor who works with Armitage and Morgan; he helps prepare and use the visibility method.
Francis MorganDoctor and scholar who joins Armitage and Rice in the investigation and final pursuit.
The hidden HorrorThe invisible second being kept at the Whateley farm; its identity is revealed only at the end.
NecronomiconFictional forbidden book. Wilbur needs the complete Miskatonic copy because his own version lacks the needed passage.
Dr. Dee English versionWilbur’s incomplete home copy of the Necronomicon.
Olaus Wormius Latin versionThe more complete Miskatonic copy that contains the passage Wilbur needs.
Wilbur’s diaryCoded record that lets Armitage reconstruct Wilbur’s plan and identify possible countermeasures.
Page 751The missing passage that turns Wilbur’s search for knowledge into a concrete plot problem.
Powder of Ibn-GhaziA visibility aid used to make the invisible Horror briefly perceptible; it is not the final destructive formula.
Sentinel HillRitual location that links Old Whateley’s prophecy, the family mystery and the climax.

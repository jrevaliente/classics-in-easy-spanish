Conflict Timeline

Spoiler alert. The later chapter headings identify major story developments. Each entry follows the real 30-chapter adaptation, not the original 21-act division.

Chapters 1–5: Desire and rejection

1. EL HALCÓN Y EL RECHAZO

Calisto follows his hawk, encounters Melibea, speaks of desire, and is rejected. The story begins with a refusal, not an agreement.

2. LA HABITACIÓN A OSCURAS

At home, Calisto isolates himself in darkness and calls Sempronio. The servant's response reveals both the master's obsession and an opening for influence.

3. EL NOMBRE DE CELESTINA

Sempronio introduces the possibility of Celestina's intervention. A private longing becomes an arrangement involving other people's interests.

4. EL AVISO DE PÁRMENO

Pármeno warns Calisto about Celestina's practices and reputation. His caution competes with his master's desperate hope.

5. LA ESPERA Y EL CONSEJO

Calisto pays for assistance before he knows the result. Impatience and money give the intermediary room to act.

Chapters 6–10: Celestina’s negotiations

6. EL HILADO Y EL CONJURO

Celestina prepares spun thread and performs an incantation. Her belief in the ritual must be distinguished from any proven supernatural effect.

7. A LA PUERTA DE MELIBEA

At Melibea's house, Celestina uses a plausible errand to gain access. The household's changing circumstances create an opportunity for a private conversation.

8. EL NOMBRE QUE CAMBIA TODO

Speaking Calisto's name changes the discussion with Melibea. Celestina seeks a personal object, but its meaning remains open to interpretation.

9. LA NOTICIA COMO MERCANCÍA

Celestina presents her progress as valuable news. Sempronio and Calisto may hear certainty where there is still uncertainty.

10. EL CORDÓN Y LAS PROMESAS

Calisto responds intensely to the cordon, while Celestina uses promises to strengthen her influence over the people around him.

Chapters 11–16: Promises and divided loyalties

11. EL PRECIO DE UN CONSEJO

Celestina works on Pármeno's resistance through appeals to loyalty, advantage and personal desire. His earlier warning begins to weaken.

12. LA NOCHE DE AREÚSA

Pármeno's meeting with Areúsa complicates his loyalties. Desire and obligation become entangled with the plans of others.

13. DOS CRIADOS, UN SOLO INTERÉS

Pármeno and Sempronio move toward a shared interest. The servants' cooperation changes the balance around Calisto.

14. LA MESA DE CELESTINA

At Celestina's table, the group reveals resentment, hierarchy and competing views of Melibea. Lucrecia's arrival brings a new request.

15. UN DESEO QUE NO PUEDE OCULTARSE

Melibea struggles with her desire and speaks to Celestina. Her own feelings and decisions must not be flattened into a simple magical explanation.

16. UNA CITA ENTRE PUERTAS

A secret meeting is arranged. What Celestina reports to Calisto is not the same as Melibea speaking directly to him.

Chapters 17–21: Secrets and costs

17. LA CADENA DE ORO

Calisto rewards Celestina with a gold chain. A gift becomes the object at the center of an emerging dispute about payment.

18. PALABRAS ENTRE PUERTAS

Calisto and Melibea speak across closed doors, with servants nearby. They agree to meet again; this is not yet the later garden encounter.

19. EL REPARTO IMPOSIBLE

Sempronio and Pármeno demand a share of Celestina's reward. Her refusal leads to violence, her death and the intervention of justice.

20. LA NOTICIA DE LA PLAZA

News of the servants' public punishment reaches Calisto. His response shows the collision of grief, reputation and his continuing desire.

21. LA NOCHE DEL HUERTO

Calisto and Melibea meet in the garden. The chapter requires attention to social pressure, Melibea's own words and the consequences of secrecy.

Chapters 22–26: Revenge and new plans

22. EL DUELO Y LA VENGANZA

Elicia and Areúsa react to the deaths and turn their grievances toward revenge. Their choices emerge from a wider network of losses and inequalities.

23. EL FUTURO QUE LOS PADRES IMAGINAN

Pleberio and Alisa consider Melibea's future without knowing her secret. Melibea overhears and tries to stop the conversation.

24. EL CRIADO QUE HABLÓ DE MÁS

Areúsa flatters Sosia and gets practical details about Calisto's visits. Elicia is hidden while the information passes between them.

25. LA PROMESA DE CENTURIO

Centurio makes bold promises but avoids direct danger. The plan he arranges is to create a disturbance, not to attack Calisto personally.

26. CANCIONES ANTES DEL PELIGRO

Melibea and Lucrecia sing while waiting. A moment of intimacy and beauty stands beside the threat approaching from outside.

Chapters 27–30: The tragic ending

27. LA ESCALA

Noise below interrupts the encounter. Calisto hurries down the ladder, falls and dies; the fall is not a direct killing by Centurio.

28. LA TORRE Y LA ÚLTIMA CONVERSACIÓN

Melibea prepares a final conversation from the tower. Her actions and her father's understanding of them are not the same.

29. LA CONFESIÓN DE MELIBEA

Melibea gives her account of the relationship and its consequences. The story does not require the reader to treat every requested outcome as completed.

30. EL PLANTO DE PLEBERIO

Pleberio laments his daughter and confronts the emptiness of wealth and worldly hopes. The tragedy ends without an invented reconciliation.

Review: what is reported, what is actually witnessed, and what is only believed?

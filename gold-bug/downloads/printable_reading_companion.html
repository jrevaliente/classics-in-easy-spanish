Printable Reading Companion

Use after reading each chapter. Write brief notes in the boxes, then compare your understanding with the book.

Chapter 1 — El escarabajo de oro

What changes after the narrator says Legrand’s drawing looks like a skull? Note what Legrand does with the parchment.

Chapter 2 — La carta de Legrand

List the details that make Jupiter and the narrator worry about Legrand. Which new tools are waiting in the boat?

Chapter 3 — Una expedición nocturna

Trace the expedition from the island to the enormous tree. What makes the narrator begin to suspect that Legrand has a real plan?

Chapter 4 — El cráneo en el árbol

Reconstruct the sequence: seventh branch → skull → left eye → cord → drop point → fifty feet.

Chapter 5 — El primer hoyo

Explain why the first hole is wrong. How can a small left/right mistake near the tree create a much larger error farther away?

Chapter 6 — El tesoro

What does finding the treasure prove? Which questions are still unanswered at the end of the chapter?

Chapter 7 — El secreto del pergamino

Reconstruct the chain of chance events that brings the hidden parchment message into view.

Chapter 8 — El mensaje cifrado

Follow Legrand’s reasoning from symbol frequency to recognizable English words. Why must the cipher remain tied to English?

Chapter 9 — La silla del diablo

Explain how Bessop’s Castle, the Devil’s Seat, the telescope, the compass and the fifty-foot line combine into one physical method.

Read Again Challenge

On your second reading, mark the exact moments when apparently accidental details become part of Legrand’s reasoning. Separate what he proves from what he only infers.

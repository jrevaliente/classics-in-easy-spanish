Clue Chain

Spoiler warning: use this page after finishing the story or while rereading it.
StepClue / actionWhy it matters
1Legrand finds the gold-coloured beetle and uses a dirty scrap to draw it.The scrap is later revealed to be parchment, not ordinary paper.
2The narrator holds the parchment close to the fire after Wolf jumps on him.Heat makes the hidden skull image begin to appear.
3Legrand later heats and cleans the parchment more carefully.A skull, a young goat / kid, and the hidden coded message emerge.
4The kid/Kidd wordplay points Legrand toward English and Captain Kidd.It gives him a plausible language for the substitution cipher.
5Legrand studies symbol frequency and repeated patterns.He reconstructs enough of the key to recover the English message.
6Bishop's hostel leads him to the local name Bessop's Castle.Bessop's Castle is a natural rock formation, not a building.
7The Devil's Seat fixes Legrand's observation position.The compass direction and telescope elevation now have a precise starting point.
8Through the telescope he identifies a skull in a very tall tree.This turns the decoded words into real geography.
9Jupiter moves to the seventh large limb on the east side and drops the cord through the skull's left eye.The drop point on the ground defines the first geometric reference.
10Legrand extends the line from the tree through the drop point by fifty feet.This determines the intended excavation area.
11The first dig fails because Jupiter has confused left and right.A small error near the tree becomes a large displacement fifty feet away.
12Legrand corrects the drop geometry and they dig again.The second excavation reveals bones, coins and finally the buried chest.

Evidence Timeline

Spoilers: use after your first reading. Revised against the repaired B1 manuscript, 27 October 2026.

1907 — Legrasse's police operation in Louisiana.

1908 — Archaeological meeting; Legrasse presents the idol and Webb recognises a connection.

28 February / 1 March 1925 — Wilcox feels the tremor and dreams in Providence on the night of 28 February. Across the International Date Line, the Pacific storm is dated 1 March. He visits Angell on 1 March.

22–23 March 1925 — The Emma encounters the Alert; the survivors reach the risen land; Wilcox's illness intensifies.

2 April 1925 — The second storm coincides with Wilcox's recovery and the end of the dreams. Thurston infers the city's renewed sinking from the Vigilant's later passage over the site.

12 April 1925 — The Vigilant finds the Alert: Johansen is alive and Briden is dead.

18 April 1925 — The Sydney Bulletin later reports the Pacific mystery.

1926–1927 — Angell dies; Thurston inherits the papers and reconstructs the chain.

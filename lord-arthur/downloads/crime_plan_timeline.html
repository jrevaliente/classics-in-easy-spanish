Crime Plan Timeline

Spoiler warning: use this after finishing the story.

Chapter I

The prediction begins

At Lady Windermere's reception, Septimus R. Podgers studies Lord Arthur's hand and sees something he does not want to explain in public.

Chapter II

Fear becomes duty

Arthur learns that murder is in his future. During a night walk through London, he moves from horror toward the belief that he must act before marrying Sybil.

Chapter III

First plan: Lady Clementina

Arthur chooses Lady Clementina as a victim, obtains poison in a capsule and presents it as medicine. He postpones the wedding and waits for news.

Chapter IV

The first plan fails

Lady Clementina dies, but Sybil later finds the capsule still intact inside the silver box. Arthur realizes that his plan did not cause the death.

Chapter V

Second plan and final act

Arthur sends an explosive clock to the Dean of Chichester. It fails absurdly. Later, beside the Thames, Arthur sees Podgers and acts directly. The official verdict is suicide.

Chapter VI

The final irony

Years later Arthur and Sybil are happily married. Lady Windermere rejects palmistry, but Arthur insists that he still believes in it—for a reason only the reader fully understands.

Character & Motive Map

Spoiler-light guide: useful while reading. The final cards contain mild plot context.

Lord Arthur Savile

Protagonist

A young aristocrat engaged to Sybil. He takes Podgers's prediction completely seriously and turns it into a question of duty.

Sybil Merton

Arthur's fiancée

She trusts Arthur even when his behaviour becomes difficult to understand. Her love gives Arthur the reason he thinks he must resolve the prophecy first.

Septimus R. Podgers

Palm reader

His reading of Arthur's hand triggers the entire chain of events. He is practical-looking, discreet and much less theatrical than the guests expect.

Lady Windermere

Society hostess

She introduces Podgers at the opening reception and later becomes part of the story's final irony.

Lady Clementina Beauchamp

Arthur's elderly relative

Witty, affectionate and rheumatic. Arthur chooses her for his first plan because he persuades himself that the choice is merciful.

Dean of Chichester

Arthur's uncle

A churchman who loves clocks. That hobby makes him the target of Arthur's second, more elaborate plan.

Herr Winckelkopf

Explosives specialist

Helps Arthur obtain the mechanism hidden inside the clock.

Jane

Family connection

Her cheerful account of the failed clock makes Arthur's second failure even more absurd.

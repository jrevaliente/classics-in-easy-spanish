Reading level and support

B1–B2 is an editorial supported-reading range, with B1 as the main reading experience and more demanding interpretation in some stories. It is not an official CEFR certification or a complete B2 grammar course.

The book contains full English translations at the end. This companion offers additional study aids rather than replacing the book.

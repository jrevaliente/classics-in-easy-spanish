Story arcs · spoilers

Conflict Timeline

24 independent short stories, not a single shared timeline. No invented dates: each arc follows a narrative tension and its change.

STORY 01 / 24

La última ilusión de don Juan

Initial tension: Don Juan idealises Estrella while courting other women.

Turning point: Estrella decides to marry.

Interpretive caution: The narrator’s defence of Don Juan is a biased perspective.

STORY 02 / 24

La inspiración

Initial tension: Poet Fausto seeks inspiration.

Turning point: He uses strangers’ tragedy for his poem.

Interpretive caution: Praise from critics does not establish personal experience.

STORY 03 / 24

Delincuente honrado

Initial tension: A prisoner calls himself honourable.

Turning point: He recounts killing his daughter.

Interpretive caution: His claim to honour is not an ethical vindication.

STORY 04 / 24

¿Justicia?

Initial tension: Pablo denies jealousy and emphasises trust.

Turning point: Stolen pearls incriminate Felicia.

Interpretive caution: Distinguish planted evidence from justice.

STORY 05 / 24

Memento

Initial tension: Gabriel joins family literary gatherings.

Turning point: Candidita’s affection becomes painfully clear.

Interpretive caution: Pity, embarrassment and affection are not interchangeable.

STORY 06 / 24

La caja de oro

Initial tension: The gold box inspires curiosity.

Turning point: An analysis reveals its contents.

Interpretive caution: Scientific findings cannot explain away human suffering.

STORY 07 / 24

El corazón perdido

Initial tension: The narrator finds a beating heart.

Turning point: A girl accepts it and later dies.

Interpretive caution: The doctors’ diagnosis differs from the narrator’s symbolic belief.

STORY 08 / 24

Un parecido

Initial tension: Marcelo longs for unavailable Jacinta.

Turning point: He finds Manuelita looks like her.

Interpretive caution: Resemblance is not identity.

STORY 09 / 24

La novia fiel

Initial tension: Amelia waits through a long engagement.

Turning point: She ends it despite loving Germán.

Interpretive caution: Separate knowledge from suspicion and unequal expectations.

STORY 10 / 24

Afra

Initial tension: An officer hears stories about Afra and Flora.

Turning point: Flora’s death provokes conjecture.

Interpretive caution: No murder has been proven.

STORY 11 / 24

La novela de Raimundo

Initial tension: A young mother attracts attention.

Turning point: Her later death prompts rumours.

Interpretive caution: Do not treat prejudice or speculation as evidence.

STORY 12 / 24

Maldición de gitana

Initial tension: An insult provokes a curse.

Turning point: A hunting tragedy seems to fulfil it.

Interpretive caution: Coincidence does not prove supernatural cause.

STORY 13 / 24

La bicha

Initial tension: Natalia is judged at Carnaval.

Turning point: Relationships and loss unsettle those judgements.

Interpretive caution: Social rumours do not establish someone’s character.

STORY 14 / 24

Apólogo

Initial tension: Vicente’s jealousy threatens Laura.

Turning point: Laura offers a cautionary story.

Interpretive caution: An analogy is not proof of a future action.

STORY 15 / 24

Martina

Initial tension: Martina’s prospective marriage is troubled by the past.

Turning point: A letter and later encounter change her choice.

Interpretive caution: Love and refusing betrayal may coexist.

STORY 16 / 24

A secreto agravio...

Initial tension: Riopardo suspects betrayal in his shop.

Turning point: He sets a fire that kills two people.

Interpretive caution: Neighbours’ sympathy contradicts the reader’s knowledge.

STORY 17 / 24

La aventura del ángel

Initial tension: An angel must spend a year on Earth.

Turning point: A human encounter changes his outlook.

Interpretive caution: The earthly memory complicates his return.

STORY 18 / 24

Así y todo...

Initial tension: Carmona disputes rules about female reputation.

Turning point: A story of violence and remarriage challenges easy explanations.

Interpretive caution: Allegation, confession and fact must stay distinct.

STORY 19 / 24

El fantasma

Initial tension: A student mistakes looks for invitation.

Turning point: Other testimonies change his reading.

Interpretive caution: Attraction and accusation remain uncertain.

STORY 20 / 24

Los buenos tiempos

Initial tension: An old portrait looks virtuous.

Turning point: A dark family history challenges that image.

Interpretive caution: Appearances are not evidence of innocence.

STORY 21 / 24

Sara y Agar

Initial tension: Three portraits reveal unusual kinship ties.

Turning point: Romana’s care brings sacrifice and loss.

Interpretive caution: The final remark leaves motives ambiguous.

STORY 22 / 24

Remordimiento

Initial tension: The viscount once believed his choice generous.

Turning point: He regrets directing his niece’s marriage.

Interpretive caution: His retrospective account is not neutral testimony.

STORY 23 / 24

Más allá

Initial tension: Two patients live beside each other without meeting.

Turning point: In the fantastic ending, the souls resist separation.

Interpretive caution: St Elmo’s fire serves a symbolic, supernatural conclusion.

STORY 24 / 24

Sor Aparición

Initial tension: A visitor encounters an elderly penitent nun.

Turning point: Others recount her earlier life.

Interpretive caution: Her true motive for penance is not established.

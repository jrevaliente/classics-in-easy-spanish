CHAPTER 01Speak Spanish Like a Local — Spain

COMPARTIR PISO SIN MALENTENDIDOS

Sharing an Apartment Without Misunderstandings

Before listening: What is at stake?

Nora has answered an advertisement for a room in Madrid. Inés, who already lives in the apartment, has invited her to see it. Nora likes the room, but she has a work meeting tomorrow morning and needs to ask about the household routine. Their first decisions may determine whether they can live together.

My goal for this scene

Negotiate a meeting time and place; decline one suggestion with a reason and confirm an alternative.

Six expressions to practise

  1. ¿Te viene bien...? — Is it convenient for you? / Does ... work for you?
  2. Me viene fatal. — That is really inconvenient for me. / That time doesn't work at all.
  3. Por mí, ... — As far as I'm concerned / I'm fine with that.
  4. Sin problema. — No problem / That's fine.
  5. Dejarlo para otro día. — Leave it for another day / postpone it.
  6. Quedar en (un sitio). — Agree to meet at a place, rather than remain somewhere.

Second listen: Which two expressions could you use in a different situation?

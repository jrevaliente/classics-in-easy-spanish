Conflict Timeline

24 chapter contexts, from the first apartment visit to the community centre's open day. Contexts and learning goals follow the manuscript.

Each entry describes the situation entering the dialogue; it does not replace reading the chapter or invent a final outcome.

Chapter 1

COMPARTIR PISO SIN MALENTENDIDOS

Sharing an Apartment Without Misunderstandings

Nora has answered an advertisement for a room in Madrid. Inés, who already lives in the apartment, has invited her to see it. Nora likes the room, but she has a work meeting tomorrow morning and needs to ask about the household routine. Their first decisions may determine whether they can live together.

Practice focus: Negotiate a meeting time and place; decline one suggestion with a reason and confirm an alternative.

Chapter 2

EL PRIMER LUNES

The First Monday

On Monday, Nora visits La Azotea, the community center where she will help create material for an open-day event. Elena needs a clear plan, but a volunteer is missing and several files have not arrived. Nora is tempted to offer more time than she can realistically give.

Practice focus: Give a realistic progress update; offer a defined amount of help and state your availability.

Chapter 3

UN CALENDARIO IMPOSIBLE

A Calendar That Won't Cooperate

The team discovers that the date on a draft poster does not match the booking calendar. Elena calls Dani and Nora together. The mistake has not reached the public yet, but everyone wants to know whether it is too late to correct it.

Practice focus: Resolve conflicting dates; agree how to notify others before the next deadline.

Chapter 4

CUENTAS CLARAS

Clear Accounts

Nora and Inés have agreed that Nora will move into the apartment the following week. Now they need to discuss shared household expenses. They also discuss Elena’s offer of paid design work at the center; Nora wants to avoid committing money she has not yet earned.

Practice focus: Compare household expenses; explain a budget limit and summarize a fair agreement.

Chapter 5

UN LOCAL CON POSIBILIDADES

A Space with Potential

Mateo takes Nora and Elena around the center before they decide what the open day can include. The courtyard looks promising, but access and noise need checking. A realistic plan matters more than a spectacular one. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Separate a promising first impression from an unconfirmed detail; arrange a practical check.

Chapter 6

LA INVITACIÓN

The Invitation

The team wants local neighbors and business owners to participate in the event. Inés proposes inviting a friend to run a workshop, but Elena is concerned that the invitation might sound like an obligation. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Invite somebody without pressure; respond to preferences and distinguish interest from confirmation.

Chapter 7

EL MENSAJE EQUIVOCADO

The Wrong Message

An enthusiastic announcement goes into the volunteer group before the workshop has been confirmed. The time is correct, but the activity is not. Dani fears that the others will blame him and hesitates to explain. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Identify a misleading announcement; acknowledge an oversight and communicate a correction.

Chapter 8

UNA BROMA A DESTIEMPO

A Joke at the Wrong Moment

With posters nearly ready, Dani tries to cheer everyone up by joking about Nora's perfectionism. Nora is already nervous about speaking in Spanish in public. A brief conversation changes the mood. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Distinguish teasing from a serious statement; explain its impact and agree an interpersonal boundary.

Chapter 9

EL METRO NO ESPERA

The Metro Won't Wait

Nora and Mateo have an appointment with a printing supplier. One of them is late, and the other worries that the supplier will leave. They need to give a realistic update instead of guessing an arrival time. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Report a delay honestly; propose a plan that protects the other person’s time.

Chapter 10

MESA PARA SEIS

A Table for Six

The team wants to meet for dinner to review progress, but their schedules don't align. Elena suggests using one evening for both a meal and a short work discussion, provided nobody feels obliged to spend money. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Reschedule a group meal; check preferences and distinguish a provisional plan from a reservation.

Chapter 11

EL CONTACTO PERDIDO

A Missing Contact

Elena remembers a photographer who documented earlier events at the center. She wants to invite her back but cannot find her details. Nora and Dani help reconstruct the clues without publishing anyone's personal information. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Explain a memory gap; request a useful cue and choose a temporary way to proceed.

Chapter 12

PRESUPUESTO BAJO LUPA

A Closer Look at the Budget

The printer's quote arrives higher than expected. Mateo convenes a short meeting before anyone approves the order. Nora proposes reducing the print run; Elena wants to keep information accessible without creating unnecessary waste. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Compare quantities and prices; adjust an order without losing essential requirements.

Chapter 13

UNA PALABRA MAL ENTENDIDA

A Word Misunderstood

Elena tells Nora that a public message needs to be shorter. Nora hears a criticism of her abilities, not the text. Inés helps them repair the misunderstanding before it affects their work. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Repair a misunderstanding; apologize for a reaction and ask a precise clarifying question.

Chapter 14

PEDIR PERMISO SIN MIEDO

Asking Permission with Confidence

The team wants to display a temporary installation in the entrance. Mateo needs to check accessibility and the center's conditions. Nora proposes alternatives that preserve the idea without forcing a yes. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Ask permission; explain a constraint and negotiate a workable alternative.

Chapter 15

UN EQUIPO PEQUEÑO

A Small Team

A volunteer cancels two days before a rehearsal. Elena and Dani debate whether to remove an activity or simplify it. The group works out what people can realistically do rather than expecting heroic last-minute effort. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: State how much help is needed; reduce a workload and allocate realistic tasks.

Chapter 16

LA REUNIÓN DECISIVA

The Decisive Meeting

Before final preparations, the team must choose between two schedules. Both have supporters. Elena wants a fair exchange and an explicit record so that nobody leaves believing a different decision was made. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Express and explain disagreement; record an explicit decision and the responsibilities it creates.

Chapter 17

QUE SE ENTERE EL BARRIO

Getting the Word Out

The event is finally confirmed. Elena asks the team to publicize it without exaggerating what the center offers. Nora designs a concise announcement; Dani talks with local businesses and discovers that word of mouth can work better than a spectacular slogan.

Practice focus: Describe an activity accurately; invite participation without treating apparent interest as a measured fact.

Chapter 18

LOS NERVIOS DEL ESTRENO

Nerves Before the Opening

Nora has been asked to introduce the exhibition in Spanish. She can explain the work, but fears forgetting her words in front of the visitors. Inés helps her rehearse, and Elena suggests a backup plan that does not turn the presentation into a test.

Practice focus: Explain nervousness; rehearse a pause and a backup prompt to resume speaking.

Chapter 19

SIN UN MINUTO LIBRE

Not a Minute to Spare

The day before the open event, everyone is busy. A sequence of small requests begins to distract Nora from an essential design check. She learns to state priorities and protect the last hour needed to finish the materials. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: State competing priorities; request uninterrupted time and agree a specific division of work.

Chapter 20

UN FALLO DE ÚLTIMA HORA

A Last-Minute Failure

On the morning of the event, the projector fails during a test. The team has printed materials but expected to use the screen for a key presentation. Dani identifies the cause, Mateo protects access to the equipment and Nora offers a solution that doesn't rely on technical miracles.

Practice focus: Report a fault without assigning blame; accept one responsibility and explain a backup plan.

Chapter 21

DESPUÉS DEL SUSTO

After the Scare

The projector works, but the opening is crowded and the reception table has to be moved. Instead of blaming anyone, the team adjusts the visitor route and offers Nora enough time to settle before her presentation. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Distinguish a small adjustment from a restart; ask for time and agree how to continue.

Chapter 22

LA CONVERSACIÓN PENDIENTE

The Conversation Nora Has Been Avoiding

After her welcome speech, Nora receives an offer to extend a freelance contract in Madrid. She is pleased but has not yet decided. She tells Inés and Elena because her availability may change, without asking them to make the decision for her.

Practice focus: Share an uncertain future change; separate informing others from committing to a decision.

Chapter 23

LA ÚLTIMA COMPROBACIÓN

The Final Check

Later that afternoon, with visitors circulating through the center, Elena and Mateo review what has worked and what can still be improved before the closing activity. Nora sees that even imperfect preparation can be worthwhile when the team listens. Read for what each person needs, what remains uncertain, and the practical decision they can make together.

Practice focus: Evaluate a result; distinguish reported information from checked facts and propose a next step.

Chapter 24

LAS PUERTAS ABIERTAS

The Doors Are Open

As the event ends, the five adults gather in the courtyard. They have handled misunderstandings, delays and uncertainty. Nora has not made every decision about her future, but she can now explain what she wants and listen to others without automatically agreeing.

Practice focus: Reflect on a shared experience; preserve useful information and close a conversation without inventing a date.

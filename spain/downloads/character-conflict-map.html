Who wants what?

Nora Blake

US designer, 33

What they are trying to do

Find a home, contribute responsibly, understand pragmatic spoken Spanish and decide what to do about future work in Madrid.

Their recurring tension

Saying yes too often, misunderstandings, workload and uncertainty about work and housing.

Connections: Inés (shared flat), Elena (project work), Dani (communications), Mateo (logistics).

Inés Torres

Cultural management, 35

What they are trying to do

Build a clear, respectful shared household and support the team's cooperation.

Their recurring tension

Boundaries about housing, honest invitations and managing humour or personal pressure.

Connections: Nora (flatmate), Elena / Dani / Mateo (La Azotea team).

Elena Ruiz

La Azotea coordinator, 46

What they are trying to do

Run an achievable, well-organised open day with accurate information and fair responsibilities.

Their recurring tension

Tight deadline, errors on flyers, budget and responsibility without overpromising.

Connections: Nora (design), Dani (communication), Mateo (facilities), Inés (team).

Dani Martín

Developer and volunteer, 29

What they are trying to do

Keep communications and technical tasks moving quickly.

Their recurring tension

Premature announcements, missed checks, jokes that land badly and technology glitches.

Connections: Elena (planning), Nora (design/communication), Inés (workplace humour), Mateo (facilities).

Mateo Vega

Facilities and logistics, 40

What they are trying to do

Keep the centre safe, accessible and technically feasible.

Their recurring tension

Room capacity, accessibility, equipment, printing costs and unrealistic plans.

Connections: Elena (coordination), Nora (layout and design), Dani (technical delivery), Inés (team).

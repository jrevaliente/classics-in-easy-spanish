Classics in Easy Catalan · BOOK01

Story & Conflict Timeline

Ten separate tales, ten story arcs. The sequence below follows each tale’s events; it is not a shared historical chronology.

Contains endings and major spoilers. Open a story after reading it.

Chapter 1 · A2 with support

La rateta

  1. The mouse wears a blue ribbon at her window.
  2. The rooster, hen, dog and ox frighten her with their calls.
  3. She invites the softly meowing cat upstairs.
  4. The cat eats her; the expected courtship ends abruptly.

Chapter 2 · A2 with support

Barba d’or

  1. The young woman insists on a husband with a golden beard.
  2. She marries the dazzling suitor and enters his glass palace.
  3. While spinning, she drops the silver spindle.
  4. The palace shatters, revealing her husband and his companions in hell. Her later fate is not stated.

Chapter 3 · A2 with support

La flor del penical

  1. The king promises his kingdom to the son who brings the healing flower.
  2. The younger son finds it; the older son steals it and buries him.
  3. A shepherd’s reed flute sings the hidden truth to three listeners.
  4. The younger son is found alive; the king condemns the older son to death and gives the kingdom to the younger.

Chapter 4 · A2 with support

La ventafocs

  1. Millet / almond / gold dress: the old woman helps; the prince sees the girl at Mass.
  2. Beans / hazelnut / silver dress: the prince recognizes her on a walk.
  3. Rice / walnut / dress with bells: she attends the ball and loses a glass shoe.
  4. The shoe fits; the prince recognizes her and they marry.

Chapter 5 · A2 with support

Els tres germans

  1. Three brothers buy a telescope, a flying carpet and a healing apple.
  2. The telescope reveals their cousin’s illness; the carpet takes them to her; the apple heals her.
  3. All three claim that their object was essential; her father sets an archery test.
  4. The youngest shoots into the sun and marries the cousin. His brothers remain unhappy.

Chapter 6 · A2 with support

La bruixa del ferrer

  1. The blacksmith’s wife secretly takes the apprentice to the witches’ cave.
  2. Enchanted headgear turns him into a horse; the repeated rides exhaust him.
  3. Following a companion’s advice, he puts the headgear on the witch.
  4. The blacksmith shoes the transformed wife; her injuries lead to her confession.

Chapter 7 · A2 with support

La Fustots

  1. The youngest daughter says she loves her father like salt; he orders her death.
  2. The servants spare her life but cut off her big toe; she becomes a goose keeper in wooden clothes.
  3. A prince sees her hidden golden dress and marries her.
  4. At the saltless wedding meal, her father understands the comparison; the family asks forgiveness.

Chapter 8 · A2 with support

El Trist

  1. The father accepts a coral necklace in exchange for a daughter; the youngest goes to the Trist.
  2. Murky river water, silent birds and dry leaves announce three illnesses of her father.
  3. On the third visit, she ignores the bell for two days; its fading sound finally brings her back.
  4. She plucks a wilted rose, breaks the spell and discovers the Trist is a prince; they marry.

Chapter 9 · Optional B1 challenge

La perera

  1. The youngest son stays awake and follows the pear-stealing bird to a deep well.
  2. He descends through three doors, defeats a giant and kills the man sustaining the princess’s spell.
  3. His family raises the princess but leaves him below; breaking the old woman’s second fang frees her.
  4. He returns through an exit she reveals. The princess chooses her rescuer and they marry.

Chapter 10 · Optional B1 challenge

Blancaflor

  1. Blancaflor’s needles summon workers who complete the wheat task overnight.
  2. With an eagle’s help she recovers the sea ring; a lost drop of blood leaves part of her little finger missing.
  3. The prince tames the enchanted horse and identifies Blancaflor by her incomplete finger.
  4. They escape with wineskin decoys and answering spittle; the prince has chosen a slower horse.
  5. A chapel, a basket of pears, fire and a sea delay pursuit until they reach his lands, where the giant has no power. They marry.

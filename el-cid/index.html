03 · Reading routine

Read · Listen · Understand

Read the chapter in Spanish. Listen again at a comfortable pace. Consult the vocabulary list, conflict timeline or printable workbook only where useful.

El cantar de mio Cid is anonymous and written in medieval Castilian. This B2 prose adaptation is by José Revaliente. B2 is an editorial classification, not official CEFR certification. The adaptation is not a verbatim medieval text.

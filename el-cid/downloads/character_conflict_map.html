Central relationship paths

Rodrigo ↔ Alfonso: vassalage → exile → gifts → pardon → royal justice.

Elvira / Sol ↔ Carrión brothers: marriage arranged under royal authority → violence at Corpes → restitution and formal challenges.

The Cid ↔ Minaya and his companions: military loyalty → diplomacy → family protection → judicial champions.

Alfonso ↔ the champions: royal protection makes the legal outcome public, rather than private revenge.
